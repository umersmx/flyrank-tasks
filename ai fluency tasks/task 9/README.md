## 📌 Executive Summary

> *"The assignment isn't 'make images'—it's 'choose images that serve your proof and look like they belong together', and knowing when a real screenshot of your work beats anything generated."*

This repository directory contains the standalone deliverables for the FlyRank Week 3 assignment: **"Kill your darlings: Curate Your Images"**.

To stand out to engineering leaders, this image strategy replaces generic AI wallpaper with **uncompromising proof integrity**:
1. **Real Work Captures**: Every case study is proven through clean, cropped, high-legibility screenshots of actual running code (Chrome DevTools layout shift measurements, VoiceOver screen reader inspection trees, and passing Vitest terminal test runs). **Zero synthetic AI mockups**.
2. **Authentic Photography**: Personal identity uses genuine natural-light photography of Muhammad Umer. Synthetic AI avatars and waxy digital renders are strictly banned.
3. **Connective Restraint**: Background textures rely on generous whitespace (`#FAFAFA`) and precision SVG geometry matching our Identity Kit.
4. **Clinical Rejection Audit**: Four AI-generated concepts were generated, diagnosed, and ruthlessly binned, documenting exactly why flashy AI slop upstages the work and destroys credibility.
