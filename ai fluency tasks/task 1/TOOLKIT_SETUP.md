### Module 1: Foundations of Working with LLMs - Summary & Takeaways

1. **The Mental Model of LLMs**:
   - Treating LLMs not as search engines or databases, but as probabilistic reasoning engines and eager, capable junior collaborators.
   - Understanding temperature, token prediction, context windows, and boundary limitations (hallucination vs. grounded extraction).

2. **The Prompting Framework**:
   - **Role Assignment**: Giving the model a domain persona and constraints drastically reduces generic or hand-wavy outputs.
   - **Clear Objective & Format Specifications**: Demanding explicit output structures (Markdown tables, JSON, typed schemas) eliminates ambiguity.
   - **Few-Shot Prompting**: Providing concrete good/bad examples calibrates the model to exact stylistic and technical expectations.

3. **Ethical, Safe & Responsible AI Collaboration**:
   - Guarding against data leakage: Never passing confidential secrets, private API keys, or personally identifiable data into third-party cloud models.
   - Epistemic vigilance: Verifying critical outputs (runtime assertions, compilation, linting) rather than trusting fluent hallucinations.
